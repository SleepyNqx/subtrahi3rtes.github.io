FAQ

Are the packs free?

Yes. Download them from the packs page and drop the zip into your resourcepacks folder.

Which versions do they work on?

Each pack lists its supported versions on its page. Most PvP packs target 1.8 and newer.

Can I use them in videos or edit them?

You can use them in videos. Please credit me, and ask on Discord before re-uploading or editing a pack.

Can you make a custom pack?

Only if it is paid for and I am interested in the theme. Message me on Discord @w.sub with your idea and budget.

Do your packs include other people's work?

They may. If you made something in one of my packs and want it removed, message me on Discord @w.sub and I will take the pack down.
